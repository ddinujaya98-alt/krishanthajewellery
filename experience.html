Skills gained

Lessons that travel from the bench.

01

Bench skills

Sawing, filing, soldering, polishing and finishing — jewellery built by hand, one piece at a time.

02

Stone setting

Bezel, prong and flush settings — cutting the seat and matching each stone so the finished piece sits right.

03

Communication

Presenting ideas clearly, taking feedback gracefully and keeping clients updated without the jargon — quotes, sizes and timelines in writing.

04

Patience & precision

Fine work takes time — a steady hand and the patience to get a detail right rather than good enough.
