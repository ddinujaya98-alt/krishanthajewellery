How a commission runs

A simple, honest process.

01

We talk

We talk about your idea — the occasion, the person, the metal and the budget. I sketch a few directions and we agree a timeline before anything else.

02

Sketches & stones

We choose the metal and the stones together, and agree the final design, size and finish. No surprises — every detail is confirmed in writing.

03

Bench work

The piece is made by hand — sawing, soldering, setting and polishing. You get steady updates, and your finished piece in a presentation box.
