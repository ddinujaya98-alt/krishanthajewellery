About

Made to be treasured.

I believe a good piece of jewellery is felt before it is understood. Every commission starts with listening — to the person, the occasion, and the story the piece needs to carry.

From a first pencil sketch to the final polish, I design and hand-make pieces in gold and silver — pairing traditional bench skills with a clean, modern eye and a real love of good stones.
