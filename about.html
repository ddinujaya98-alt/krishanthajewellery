About me

Made to be treasured.

I'm a jewellery designer and craftsman who cares about the space between beauty and meaning — where a piece stops being an object and starts carrying a memory.

Introduction

Hello — nice to meet you.

I'm a jewellery designer and craftsman based in Sri Lanka, working with gold, silver and stones from every corner of the world.

It started with a simple fascination: how a flat sheet of metal and a rough stone can become something people keep for a lifetime. That curiosity grew into a practice — sawing, filing, soldering, setting and polishing, learned piece by piece at the bench.

Today I make bespoke jewellery to order — engagement and wedding rings, bridal sets, and repairs that give old pieces a second life. Every commission is a chance to make something warmer, more personal and made to last.

— Krishantha Balacharige

My approach

The approach behind the work.

Three principles guide every piece I make — from a simple band to a full bridal set.

01

Comfort first

Before anything looks beautiful, it has to sit and wear well. I start with the person — how the piece will be worn and how it should feel on the hand.

02

Honest materials

Real gold and silver, stones chosen and matched by hand. What you are told you are getting is exactly what you get.

03

Craft the details

The setting, the solder line, the final polish — the small decisions are what separate a good piece from one you keep for life.

Strengths

What I bring to the table.

  • Bench skills. Sawing, filing, soldering, polishing and finishing — jewellery made by hand, not assembled from parts.
  • Stone knowledge. Choosing, matching and setting stones — cuts, colours and proportions that sit right together.
  • Design sense. Proportion, wearability and a clean, modern eye — pieces that look as good in person as on paper.
  • Clear communication. Honest timelines, steady updates and no jargon — just progress you can see.
  • Patience & precision. Fine work takes time — a steady hand and the patience to get a detail right.
→

Working principles

Listen before making. Show work early. Keep the piece honest. Respect the deadline. Finish what I start.

→

Who I work with

Couples, families, jewellers and anyone who wants something personal — an engagement ring, a gift, or an heirloom brought back to life.

Off the bench
